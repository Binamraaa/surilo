Tap a note to edit it

Drag a note up or down to fix a flat or sharp note, or tap it and use the buttons. Drag a selected note's edges to change its length. Double-click an empty spot to add a note (press and hold on a phone). The grey line is your actual voice. Dashed notes (≈) are best guesses: you hummed between two notes, so the app picked the one that fits the tune. Tap one to check it.
